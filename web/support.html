Support

ZetaReps is free and no longer actively maintained

Version 1.0.4 is the final release. Every feature is unlocked for everyone, and installed copies keep working after the App Store listing is gone. The source code is published on GitHub under the MIT license, so anyone can build or improve it.

Common questions

Reminders do not appear. Check Settings, Notifications, ZetaReps in iOS and make sure notifications are allowed, then confirm a schedule is set under the app's Settings tab.

Scores show no value. Consistency and intensity need at least three completed workouts within the selected time range.

Where is my data? On your device only. Nothing is uploaded, so deleting the app deletes the data; there is no account to recover it from.

I paid for Premium. The subscription was removed from sale in October 2026 and nothing renews. Refund requests for an unused period go through Apple's Report a Problem page.

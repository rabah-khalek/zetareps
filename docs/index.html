Features.

Track your push-up workouts with set-based training, performance metrics, and smart notifications. All data stored on your device.

Set-Based Tracking

Track sets, reps, and rest periods with automatic rest timer between sets.

Performance Metrics

Consistency & Intensity scores (0-10) track your workout frequency and quality.

Fitness Level Targets

Choose your fitness level for auto-calculated workout targets.

Smart Notifications

Context-aware reminders respect rest days and recent workouts.

Streak Tracking

Track consistency with rest day support and 2-day gap tolerance.

Progress Charts

Visualize metrics & reps with scrollable charts and performance bands.

Difficulty Rating

Rate each workout on a 1-5 scale to track intensity trends.

Rest Timer

Automatic rest periods between sets with skip option for flexibility.

Personal Records

Track your best workout, longest streak, and total pushups.

The source code is published under the MIT license, so anyone can build it with Xcode or improve it.

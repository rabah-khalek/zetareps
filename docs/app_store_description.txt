Build Your Push-Up Habit with ZetaReps

ZetaReps is the simplest way to track push-up workouts and build consistency. No complicated setup—just start tracking and let the app guide you.

CORE FEATURES

Set-Based Training
Track your workouts with sets, reps, and rest periods. Complete workouts are automatically logged with duration tracking.

Performance Metrics
Get Consistency and Intensity scores (0-10) that track your workout frequency and exercise quality. Visualize your progress with detailed charts.

Smart Targets
Choose your fitness level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) for auto-calculated workout targets based on scientific recommendations.

Smart Notifications
Receive context-aware workout reminders that respect rest days and recent workouts. Fully customizable scheduling.

Progress Tracking
See your workout history with visual charts showing metrics trends and rep counts over time. Track your streak and personal records.

Rest Timer
Automatic rest periods between sets with the option to skip when ready. Built-in timer keeps you on track.

Personal Records
Track your best workout, longest streak, and total pushups completed. Celebrate your achievements.

PRIVACY FIRST
All your workout data is stored securely on your device. We don't collect or share any personal information.

COMPLETELY FREE
Everything is included for everyone: unlimited pushups and workouts, full history, all analytics charts, the complete calendar and all-time statistics. There is no subscription and nothing to unlock.
